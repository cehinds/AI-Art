## Deliverables and grid contract

Five opaque source atlases, each 1983×793 RGB (near 2.5:1), arranged as five columns by two rows of nominally square cells. The exporter contract is recorded in [sheets.json](../ArtSource/RemasterExpansion/biomes-natural/sheets.json): 32×32 target tile, 32 PPU, centered pivot, group `tiles`. Each sheet uses the following row-major names: `nw`, `n`, `ne`, `w`, `center`, `e`, `sw`, `s`, `se`, `center-alt`.

## Full generation prompts

Each image was generated with `transparent_background: false`. The complete prompt for each sheet is the shared prompt below followed by its family-specific paragraph.

### Shared prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: native-ready candidate source atlas for a top-down JRPG remake. Create one wide horizontal 5:2 atlas, ideally exactly 2500×1000 pixels, with exactly five equal square columns and two equal square rows (ten square tiles total, each 500×500 source pixels). No margins. Invisible boundaries only: absolutely no drawn grid, cell frames, gutters, dividers, borders, labels, numbers, watermark, or tile artwork crossing cell boundaries. Every cell is an independent full-bleed tile crop. This is a practical terrain autotile atlas, not a map illustration. Artwork style: crisp GBA-era FFII Pixel Remaster-inspired pixel clusters, clean chunky 16/32-bit clusters that remain legible at 32×32, muted natural palette, consistent upper-left light, orthographic top-down ground plane; short top-down-oblique side faces only on raised terrain edges. Keep broad color shapes, avoid painterly detail and noisy microtexture. Ten cells in exact row-major source positions: row 1 [nw, n, ne, w, center]; row 2 [e, sw, s, se, center-alt]. The nine named edge/corner cells represent a 3×3 raised vegetation/terrain patch: each named corner/side has baseground visible outside the raised patch on the appropriate outer edge, edge elevations and material boundaries must match adjacent tiles, and center is fully covered with the biome surface; center-alt is a second fully covered repeatable interior surface.

### grassland-v2

> Biome: grassland-v2. Build a grassy turf plateau patch with fresh green grass surface and a low earthen escarpment along its perimeter; exposed earth baseground outside the correct named sides is warm tan-brown compacted soil. Use irregular natural turf edges, soil face only at outer edges, and matching corner pieces that form a clean connected raised meadow in the 3×3 semantic arrangement. The nw, n, ne, w, e, sw, s, se tiles must keep the grass top plane and earth edge at matching heights when assembled. center and center-alt are fully grass-covered, subtly different green meadow repeats with only sparse tiny flower flecks. No trees, shrubs as objects, boulders, roads, water, characters or props.

### temperate-forest

> Biome: temperate-forest. Build a connected raised patch of dense temperate woodland undergrowth over brighter green meadow baseground. The raised patch surface is dark olive leaf litter, moss and small fern-like floor cover; its outer edge has a coherent soft scalloped fringe of low vegetation above a short dark earth face. The baseground must show outside the correctly named north/west/east/south/corner edges. Keep all nine edge/corner pieces geometrically compatible in the semantic 3×3 arrangement. center and center-alt are fully filled walkable forest-floor repeats, subtly varied leaf litter and moss. Forest canopy shadows may graze edges, but no whole tree silhouettes, trunks, flowers as props, logs, rocks, characters or objects.

### conifer-highlands

> Biome: conifer-highlands. Build a connected raised alpine plateau of muted evergreen moss and short groundcover, with a dark slate-gray exposed rock baseground outside its perimeter. The nine edge/corner cells form a coherent low rocky shelf: pale upper-left-lit granite rim, short charcoal stone side-face on the correct exposed edge, matching corner intersections, and a filled top surface with sparse fir needles, lichen and hardy grass. center and center-alt are fully covered highland plateau repeats with subtle moss/stone variation. Keep the palette cool gray, sage, muted pine green; no snowfield, tall trees, standalone conifer sprites, mountainscape panorama, cliffs taller than one tile, characters, signs or objects.

### swamp-marsh

> Biome: swamp-marsh. Build a coherent slightly raised marsh hummock patch against dark blue-green shallow water and wet mud baseground, visible outside the correctly named outer edges/corners. The nine boundary cells show an organic low mossy bank with wet soil sides and subtle water lapping at the outer edge; corners meet cleanly, side banks align, center is fully filled with dark wetland turf. center-alt is another fully filled marsh-floor repeat, same waterlogged soil and sparse low sedge tufts. Use olive reeds as small ground texture, muddy umber, muted teal water, soft algae specks. Do not draw isolated tall reed props, cattails, trees, logs, rocks, buildings, characters, or pond scene.

### tropical-jungle

> Biome: tropical-jungle. Build a connected raised island of dense low jungle groundcover above dark wet brown soil baseground; the outer baseground is visible on each correctly named side and corner outside the patch. The nine edge/corner pieces use layered emerald and deep jade broad-leaf ground vegetation, with a low vine/leaf fringe and short dark earth sides; each named corner and side fits the same 3×3 raised patch and meets its neighbors. Center and center-alt are fully covered jungle-floor repeats: rich dark leaf litter, moist soil, small moss and broad-leaf pixel clusters, subtly varied. Humid saturated green palette with restrained highlights. No tall tree sprites, trunks, canopy panorama, isolated plants, flowers, characters, buildings, props or water features.


## v2 cleanup generation and review status

After native 32×32 proofs exposed black straight dividers in v1, the five v1 sources were preserved unchanged. On 2026-10-24, each was edited with built-in ImageGen using its original source as the reference. The v2 PNGs are selected in `sheets.json`; v1 files remain excluded by the adjacent exclusion ledger. Each generated edit remained 1983×793 RGB. The output keeps the 5×2 source layout and the original row-major tile identities. ImageGen removed/softened the obvious vertical atlas strokes in previews, while row transitions remain because the tile artwork itself varies across that row. The updated native proofs must be checked for actual divider absence and joined-edge quality before final acceptance.

Shared v2 edit instruction: “Image edit of the supplied atlas, not a new scene. Preserve the existing biome materials, colors, natural terrain silhouettes and tile positions. The source has thin straight dark atlas-grid marks at cell boundaries; remove these artificial straight lines and inpaint the obscured material texture so no black/dark straight line crosses any tile. Do not redraw separators. Keep the 5-column by 2-row layout and exact row-major cells nw,n,ne,w,center / e,sw,s,se,center-alt, each square and full-bleed. Preserve style/colors/composition; no props or redesign. Exact requirement: no artificial straight dark grid marks in the final image. Opaque crisp 16/32-bit JRPG terrain, upper-left light, top-down ground.”

Family-specific preservation clauses appended to that instruction:

- `grassland-v2`: “Preserve the existing grassland meadow and earth colors and all irregular natural terrain silhouettes. Reconstruct any material obscured by the dividers.”
- `temperate-forest`: “Preserve the existing temperate woodland undergrowth, leaf litter, moss, meadow baseground, and all organic raised terrain edges. Reconstruct the forest floor and terrain texture beneath them.”
- `conifer-highlands`: “Preserve the existing conifer-highland moss, stone, slate-gray baseground, and natural rocky shelf edges. Reconstruct any rock or moss texture obscured by them.”
- `swamp-marsh`: “Preserve the existing swamp marsh hummock, shallow teal water, muddy baseground, and organic raised bank edges. Reconstruct any soil, water, moss, or marsh texture obscured by them.”
- `tropical-jungle`: “Preserve the existing dense low jungle groundcover, dark wet soil baseground, and irregular raised leaf-fringe edges. Reconstruct leaves and soil texture obscured by them.”
