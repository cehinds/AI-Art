# WorldBuilder100 construction guide

This collection contains **exactly100 new32×32 tiles in ten biome sets**, plus **32 separately counted static object sprites**:16 vehicle direction views,8 trees and8 mountain/cave landmarks. The132 individual PNGs are separate from the review-only packed atlas. Vehicle views are static choices; this collection adds no animation clips. Sky material and the fantasy propeller-plane are optional extensions.

## Files and coordinates

- inventory.json lists every actual tile/object ID, repository PNG path, SHA-256 and portablePath.
- tiles-100-native-atlas.png is a320×320 packed copy: ten columns by ten biome rows, each cell exactly32×32. atlas-coordinates.json maps every cell to its individual PNG. Rows sort biome owner keys lexically; columns follow nw,n,ne,w,center,e,sw,s,se,center-alt. Coordinates include top-left pixel y and Unity bottom-origin unityY.
- Portable archive paths are tiles/{owner}/{name}.png and sprites/{owner}/{name}.png. rootmanifest.json, when packaged by the coordinator, maps IDs to archive paths. The project gallery, source images and test-scene PNGs live outside that portable package unless separately included; links to project review pages are not portable package contents.

## Build a rectangular patch

1. Keep tiles at32 PPU, point filtered, uncompressed and without mipmaps. Each32px tile occupies one world unit. These are individual Sprite PNGs, not preconfigured TileBase or RuleTile assets.
2. Place nw/ne/sw/se at the four outer corners. Repeat n/s along the top/bottom, and w/e along the left/right. Fill the interior with center. center-alt is an optional interior variation; compare it visually before mixing it into a region.
3. The supplied5×5 test scenes use exactly those nine roles from actual exports. They show source texture repetition and boundary issues plainly; they are not seamlessness claims or reconstructed original maps.
4. Place64px trees/landmarks and128px vehicles at the same32 PPU. Their canvases therefore occupy2×2 and4×4 tile units respectively. Respect each declared pivot: bottom center for props and center for vehicles. Transparent margins mean the visible body may be smaller than its canvas.
5. Choose direction down/left/right/up explicitly for each vehicle. Do not cycle those views as if they were motion frames.

## Collision suggestions only

Object entries contain a suggested native-pixel rectangle and Unity-local AABB. Tree suggestions cover the trunk/base; mountain suggestions cover a lower obstacle footprint; cave suggestions identify an entrance trigger that still needs manual solid geometry around its opening; vehicle suggestions identify a hull or boarding zone. **No collision, trigger, navigation, walkability or scene behavior is implemented.** Review bounds against the intended game and actual art. A tile's cardinal art role does not establish which pixels are traversable.

## Missing construction coverage

This is ten material sets, not all possible biomes. Inner/concave corners, complete cross-biome transition combinations, diagonal shore slopes, bank variations, cliffs/elevation, full bridge approaches, seasons, terrain rules and map-specific event objects remain separate work. Visible divider-line remnants, repeated motifs, color steps and mismatched joins must be repaired or replaced before claiming a seamless production map kit. No pixels were painted to hide these issues in the test scenes.

The offline project gallery is Review/remaster-expansion/worldbuilder100/index.html. Construction previews and suggested bounds are review artifacts only; browser policy checks, Unity import, Unity collision setup and playable runtime were not performed for this collection.
