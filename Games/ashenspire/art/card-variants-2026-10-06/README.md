# Alternate card illustrations

Eight distinct 1024 x 1536 paintings extend the owner's shield, fiery weapon and
cloaked swordsman references: Frost Aegis, Storm Spear, Emerald Ward, Cinder Axe,
Void Crescent, Golden Hammer, Frost Lunge and Ashen Bow.

`prompts.json` and individual `receipts/` entries preserve built-in OpenAI image
generation prompts, IDs and SHA-256 hashes. Original PNGs remain in `masters/`.
First-party generated artwork; no third-party asset license is claimed.

The Card Assembler exposes these as alternate artwork under the Variants class.
They do not create new gameplay mechanics or card IDs. Names, cost values, icons,
frames and rules stay separate editable layers.
