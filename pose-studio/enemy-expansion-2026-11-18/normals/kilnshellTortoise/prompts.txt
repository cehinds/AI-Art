Design
A production game battle sprite, ONE original normal enemy from the dark fantasy card RPG AshenSpire. Kilnshell Tortoise: a heavy four-legged charcoal tortoise whose shell resembles a brick-red fired-clay kiln dome with a single squat chimney. Soft orange embers show through shell vents. Low horned head, short broad feet. Full-body paused idle side-on three-quarter view facing screen LEFT, entire body and gear visible, grounded natural weight. Clean painterly shaded miniature illustration, detailed hand-painted bevels and materials, crisp readable bold silhouette, earthy subdued colors with small bright accents, rendered for 150 pixel game display. Character is alone centered with generous blank padding on all four sides; no environment, floor, drop shadow or halo. Real transparent alpha background. No text or borders. Square canvas.
