Design
A production game battle sprite, ONE original normal enemy from the dark fantasy card RPG AshenSpire. Bracken Tusker: wild shaggy moss-brown boar with two sweeping ivory tusks and a burnt-orange bracken mane, four black hooves and amber eyes; no gear. Full-body paused idle side-on three-quarter view facing screen LEFT, entire body and gear visible, grounded natural weight. Clean painterly shaded miniature illustration, detailed hand-painted bevels and materials, crisp readable bold silhouette, earthy subdued colors with small bright accents, rendered for 150 pixel game display. Character is alone centered with generous blank padding on all four sides; no environment, floor, drop shadow or halo. Real transparent alpha background. No text or borders. Square canvas.

Pose master

SUBJECT Bracken Tusker: wild shaggy moss-brown boar with two sweeping ivory tusks and a burnt-orange bracken mane, four black hooves and amber eyes; no gear. ONE uniformly spaced 4-column x 3-row sprite atlas (4 cells across, 3 down); all 12 complete figures absolutely contained in separate square cells with 14% of each cell blank on all sides, no touching, generous gutters. Full-body dark fantasy RPG painted 2D game sprites, each wholly visible. No scenery, no drop shadow, no panel dividers, no labels, no glyphs, no text, no vignette; genuinely transparent alpha outside bodies.
