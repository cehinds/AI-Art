# Compact artwork delivery — 2026-10-02

The shop-manual screenshot supplied by the owner guided the material treatment:
small, clearly shaped objects, hand-painted texture, dark leather and old brass,
and restrained color cues against the game's dark surfaces. It was a style
reference, not an edit target or an asset copied into the game.

All new master PNGs in `flasks-2026-10-02/`, `relics-2026-10-02/`,
`events-2026-10-02/`, and `portraits-2026-10-02/` were generated with the
built-in `image_gen` tool. Every distinct identity received its own prompt and
its own transparent image. The common prompt constraints were: a single
hand-painted dark-fantasy subject, tactile materials, clear silhouette at game
size, controlled highlights, transparent outer background, and no labels,
frames, UI, or unrelated props. Subjects came from the corresponding catalog
name, effect, and narrative text. The Road Warden prompt also required an
unmarked weathered coat, signal lantern, supply ledger, full body, and no crest.

Runtime item and event assets are 256×256 WebP at quality 78 with alpha quality
90. Their mobile twins are re-encoded at quality 50 with alpha quality 60, or
copied when a re-encode would be larger. The Road Warden portrait is 512×512
desktop and 256×256 mobile. Google libwebp `cwebp` 1.6.0 performed technical
resizing and compression; `tools/import-item-art.ps1` is the repeatable intake
path for further items and events. The transparent PNGs are retained as
editable-size masters outside the runtime asset tree.

The 195 card identities use the existing compact outline catalog, with reviewed
paintings taking precedence for four card IDs and three equipment profiles.
The card mobile twins preserve their 3:2 aspect ratio at 256×171 and 128×85.
The three nonempty character-creation keepsakes reuse shipped item art, adding
no new image payload. `Nothing` intentionally has no image.

After replacement of any PNG, WebP, or configured path, run
`node tools/art-inventory.mjs --write --check`,
`node tools/mobile-art.mjs --check`, the focused art tests, and both standalone
bundle builds. Review the asset in the actual choice, inventory, or dialogue
surface before calling it approved.

## Import into AshenSpire-art

This package adds 147 high-tier runtime files: 77 painted WebPs for relics,
flasks, events, and the Road Warden, plus 70 card assets (56 reusable SVG
motifs and 14 painted WebP exports). Their 147 light-tier counterparts were
generated by this repository's `tools/mobile-art.mjs`, which writes the
per-source provenance in `light/.twin-sources.json`. The source PNGs and card
authoring files are retained under `art/`; the four screenshots in
`inspection/` show the source game screen at desktop and phone sizes.

The art repository packages images only. The event, speaker, keepsake, relic,
and card lookup code stays in AshenSpire. After this art PR merges and releases,
the game must pin the new art release to make these files available from an
external-art build.
