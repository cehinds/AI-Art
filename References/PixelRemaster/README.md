## Interpretation boundary

The new strips use authored anticipation, action, impact, follow-through and recovery with explicitly chosen durations. They are inspired by the readable pose vocabulary of Pixel Remaster and the project's approved art, not measured reproductions of its frame-by-frame animation. Existing source art and old native exports remain preserved. Generated tiles require assembled adjacency checks; a tile count is not proof of seamless maps.

## Party expansion follow-up

The [official Square Enix FFII character page](https://na.finalfantasy.com/titles/finalfantasy2) was read on 2026-10-24 to confirm the remaining base-game party identities: Minwu, Josef, Gordon, Leila and Ricard. It identifies Minwu as a white mage, Josef as an ally from Salamand, Gordon as a Kashuan prince, Leila as a pirate captain and Ricard as a dragoon. This is roster and role evidence, not a frame-timing reference. Their previously approved Campaign source sheets supply the project's costume and silhouette references; the RemasterExpansion sheets supply the richer rendering style. No official illustration was downloaded or imported for this follow-up.

Snow/ice, desert ruins, ship/dock and dungeon construction tiles extend material coverage. They are reusable art studies, not verified reproductions of individual FFII map layouts.
