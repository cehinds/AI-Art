# Palette and density reference review V2

Reference-only material for the PaletteDensityPassV2 art review. This folder preserves original FFII PR captures, exact crops, measurements and comparison-board generators. It contains no game integration or runtime claims. Keep the original source pixels separate from any new assets.

## Quick visual requirements

### Overworld

- **Grass color:** the clear PR grass sample in `crops/overworld-grass.png` (source frame 02, x=200, y=130, 90×60) has mean RGB about `(106,175,55)` and median `(101,169,54)`. It reads as bright yellow/lime green. The prior V3 tile sample measured around `(50,108,82)` mean: markedly darker and bluer. Treat these as sampled values from those particular crops, not a complete palette specification.
- **River:** compare against `crops/overworld-river-winding.png`, the real FFII PR winding scene crop (frame 03, x=0, y=65, 130×205). In a straighter section the saturated blue channel spans roughly 18–22 screenshot pixels, with a thin olive/green and earth fringe; bends widen. Water should occupy a substantial part of a 32px material cell, not remain a 5–7px thread. This pixel-width interpretation is visual measurement, not proof of an engine tile pitch.
- **Natural composition:** compare both the PR winding capture and the canonical bridge scene. The 130×205 row isolates material/width; candidate path placement differs there. The 130×270 canonical tile proof and generated reconstruction use the same scene crop as the actual screenshot.
- The four terrain rows in `boards/terrain-matched-scale.png` are shown at equal native dimensions and equal nearest-neighbor display scale per row. Grass is 90×60 at 3×; the winding sample is 130×205 at 3×; canonical tile proof and separate scene reconstruction are 130×270 at 3×.
- **Grass comparison:** the coarser V4 grass-field crop mean `(105.38,179.06,54.61)` is close to the PR sample mean `(106,175,55)`. Its grain is closer in size to the screenshot than the superseded fine-grain version, though the candidate still shows more regular block/checker texture than the PR crop’s soft mottling.
- **Winding proof:** its actual water width and thin edging are visible, but it depicts a different path placement from the screenshot crop. Its woodland ground is more muted and closer in palette than the prior bright-field trial, but retains visible rectangular tile mottling; this row is for material and width review, not scene matching.
- **Canonical tile proof:** the bridge and bend align as a scene-level comparison and river width reads broadly similar. Woodland ground is closer in overall palette, though its rectangular mottling remains more regular than the PR capture. Forest crowns are denser/more regular, and the assembled water has more repeated dash texture than the source’s softer highlights.
- **Generated reconstruction:** the water rim is thinner than its first pass, and bend/channel shape tracks the capture. The surrounding grass remains denser/more speckled and the olive banks brighter. It is newly generated review art, not connector-tile assembly or a production terrain proof. Its normalized proof, provenance, and source master are copied here; the rejected cyan-edge first pass is retained under `iterations/`.

### Tobul weapon shop: locked same-scale frame

- Actual source: Vorxu's FFII PR Tobul screenshot, `sources/pr-smithy.webp` (960×540). The room crop is fixed to `(213,0,534,440)` and preserved as `crops/tobul-room-viewport.png`. This crop was checked against the original source pixels. New candidate proof must use this same 534×440 viewport. If it arrives 534×441, the board builder trims only the last bottom row and records that in its label; any other size is rejected.
- The screenshot has dominant horizontal pixel runs in multiples of three, consistent with a 3× displayed source pixel grid. This is an observed scale estimate from this capture, not a claim about FFII PR's internal rendering resolution.
- The Tobul crop shows the merchant left of center (about 28–30% of crop width), dense wall stock across several bays, a thick orange wood L-counter spanning roughly 60% of crop width, a deep red floor, a large cream/tan rug with violet outlines and diamond details, and two foreground customers. Preserve this composition and relative scale in a close recreation; avoid a large empty room with a tiny centered clerk.
- In this exact source crop, sample medians are approximately `(167,31,31)` for a flat red-floor patch, `(222,186,178)` for a cream wall panel, and `(203,186,152)` for the patterned rug center. Pattern and shading make these indicative reference samples rather than exact color targets.
- `crops/tobul-upper-room.png`, `crops/tobul-service-counter.png`, and `crops/tobul-floor-rug.png` are regions cut from the locked viewport at its original displayed scale. Their comparison rows must use the same source-relative crop rectangle on the new proof; do not enlarge a component crop or compare it with a whole room.
- The comparison boards in `boards/tobul-viewport-matched-scale.png` and `boards/tobul-components-matched-scale.png` use the normalized weapon-room proof at the locked screenshot dimensions. The room layout, shopkeeper, display bays, L-counter, red floor and rug are compared directly. The original gameplay capture contains two customers and a dialogue bubble; the static recreation omits those temporary foreground elements. This is a visible scope difference, not evidence that the candidate recreates the full gameplay screenshot.
- `boards/tobul-individual-components.png` provides six paired source-relative crops: a cream/timber wall bay, red floor, full available rug area, chest fixture, display racks, and gold L-counter context. Each pair is enlarged by the same nearest-neighbor factor, stated in the panel label. The original rug crop includes customers/dialogue at its top edge, while the static candidate does not.
- `boards/store-variants-contact-sheet.png` shows the weapon, armor, item and magic proposals, all at 534×440. Weapon is anchored to the Tobul capture. Armor, item and magic are proposed room variants; no matching original-room capture is claimed for those layouts. Snapshot hashes and source locations are in `store-proof-manifest.json`.

## Provenance and source limits

- `source-manifest.json` records original URLs, local source paths, dimensions and hashes. `reference-crop-manifest.json` records exact crop rectangles and crop hashes.
- The Tobul image is the only locally inspected shop-interior screenshot in this set. Scott's room and Hilda's scene are preserved as additional FFII PR interior references; the item-menu screenshot is battle UI, not an interior. Do not present them as proof of weapon, armor, item, magic or temple room layouts.
- The world frames come from the Vorxu Altair-to-Gatrea/Fynn walkthrough. They show actual grass, river and bridge material; they do not establish a continuous dirt-road treatment.
- `crops/ffii-pr-river.png` is a byte-for-byte copy of the attributed 130×270 canonical PR winding-river crop from the prior TerrainShopPassV1 comparison; its source rect and SHA are recorded in `reference-crop-manifest.json`.
- The broad room comparison uses identical native pixel size. The component detail and terrain boards apply the same labeled nearest-neighbor scale to each matched pair; the builders enforce identical source/candidate crop dimensions.

Production asset links point to `../../../Assets/Art/Production/ShopInteriorsV2/samples/`; verify those after the production pack is copied beside the published reference set.
